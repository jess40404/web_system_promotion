Mining Operations in the Philippines

The Philippines is positioned at the heart of a dynamic Asia-Pacific economy, with diverse mineral endowments and a growing focus on responsible resource development. Its mining sector supports livelihoods, regional infrastructure, and international supply chains.

Sibuyan Island, Romblon

Primary Mineral: Nickel

Threatens pristine watersheds and endemic biodiversity of Mt. Guiting-Guiting.

Surigao del Norte & Dinagat

Primary Mineral: Nickel Ore

Extensive strip mining causing heavy red siltation along coastal fisheries.

Tampakan, South Cotabato

Primary Mineral: Copper & Gold

One of Southeast Asia's largest undeveloped sites, threatening agricultural rivers.

Benguet & Cordillera Region

Primary Mineral: Gold & Copper

Centuries of extraction leading to severe mountain landslide vulnerabilities.

— WHY WE DEFEND NATURE

7 Negative Effects of Unchecked Mining

⚠️ 1. Job Loss

Temporary booms lead to permanent economic crashes once minerals run dry.

🐾 2. Habitat Loss

Endemic wildlife species are permanently displaced and pushed toward extinction.

💧 3. Water Pollution

Toxic runoffs poison drinking watersheds and ruin coastal fishing grounds.

💨 4. Air Pollution

Heavy mineral dust causes widespread lung diseases in nearby communities.

⛰️ 5. Soil Erosion & Landslides

Stripped mountain slopes crumble into fatal mudslides during typhoons.

🌲 6. Forest Loss

Deforestation destroys natural carbon sinks and triggers severe climate shifts.

🚫 7. Loss of Natural Resources

Irreplaceable natural wealth is extracted for short-term foreign export profit.

Inclusive Green Jobs & Industry Transition

A green transition supports everyone—offering accessible livelihoods for miners without formal schooling or degrees, while transforming mining companies into restoration enterprises.

FOR MINERS

Reforestation & Seed Nursery Specialist

Miners possess unmatched physical stamina and field terrain knowledge. They can be employed directly in native tree seed propagation, mountain soil stabilization, and ecosystem restoration.

FOR MINERS

Eco-Ranger & Forest Protection Patrol

Former miners can transition into paid community forest guards (Bantay Gubat) and marine wardens (Bantay Dagat) to protect protected watersheds against illegal activity.

FOR OPERATORS

Eco-Restoration Equipment Operators

Heavy equipment operators trained on mining excavators can repurpose their skills toward building terraced slopes, land rehabilitation, and drainage engineering for eco-parks.

FOR MINING CORPORATIONS

Corporate Ecological Rehabilitation Services

Mining entities must legally shift business models toward environmental remediation, sustainable renewable energy development, and bio-diverse agroforestry investments.

Economic Growth & Innovation Through Nature

1. Nature is Our Core Economic Engine

Protecting clean water sources allows farming, fishing, and tourism to support communities for generations, whereas a single mine can permanently destroy those lasting resources.

2. Green Innovation & Circular Economy

Rather than tearing up the earth for new raw materials, real progress comes from recycling existing metals, using natural materials, and building clean energy so we don't need to mine as much.

3. Valuing Natural Capital Over Short-Term Cash

Strong economies value natural benefits (clean air, protection from storms, and healthy soil) as real national wealth, because protecting nature ensures long-term financial security and stability.

4. Integrating Nature into Economic Planning

Future economic policies must integrate environmental sustainability, ensuring that development projects consider long-term ecological impacts and community well-being.

5. Nature's Wealth is Irreplaceable

The quick money made from selling unprocessed nickel isn't worth the permanent destruction of untouched nature that provides us with clean air, fresh water, and climate protection.

6. Solidarity Shielding

Protecting nature isn't just an idea it is local people defending their community. When residents stand together to protect their land, they become the strongest defense against environmental destruction.
