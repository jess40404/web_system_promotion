Welcome to Wildwood Sirens

Discover Baey Anito Falls, a quiet natural sanctuary in Alilem, Ilocos Sur, known as the "House of the Spirits."

Introduction About the Place

What makes us special

Baey Anito Falls features cool natural swimming pools, clear cascading waters, and scenic mountain landscapes perfect for nature lovers and adventurers seeking a peaceful getaway.

How Far

Located in Alilem, Ilocos Sur. It takes about 30 to 40 minutes to drive from Sudipen, La Union, followed by a simple 30-minute walk along a paved path.

Free

• Entrance to the falls is completely free for all visitors.

• Free access to local viewing decks and natural swimming areas.

• Minimal local transport costs apply if hiring local guide services.
